SPOUSE VISA QUIZ

Weighted scoring. Each answer has points (0-3). Max 21. Score determines result.
16-21 = "strong" / 10-15 = "challenges" / 0-9 = "expert"

Q1: "What's your relationship status with your partner?"
Legally married=3, Civil partnership=3, Living together 2+ years=2, Less than 2 years=1, Engaged not married=0, Relationship don't live together=0

Q2: "What is your current annual income (before tax)?"
£29,000+=3, £23,500-£28,999=1, Below £23,500=0, Self-employed=1, Not working=0

Q3: "Do you have savings that could support your application?"
Over £16,000=3, Less than £16,000=1, No significant savings=0, Not sure what counts=1

Q4: "Does your partner meet the English language requirement?"
English-speaking country=3, Has test/UK degree=3, Plans to take test=2, English is a barrier=0, Not sure=1

Q5: "Do you have suitable accommodation arranged in the UK?"
Own/rent with space=3, Living with family=2, Need to arrange=1, Not sure what's adequate=1

Q6: "Has your partner ever been refused a UK visa or had immigration issues?"
Clean history=3, Previous refusal=1, Overstayed=0, Ban/deportation=0, Not sure=1

Q7: "How much evidence of your relationship do you have?"
Lots=3, Some=2, Not much=1, Married quickly/short history=0
